Hot Ones stats

What drives views on guest interviews? Pace is views per day since upload, so old and new episodes compare fairly. The episode furthest above its neighbors:

Episodes over time

Length vs. pace

Views per day against video length in minutes, both on log scales.

What goes with pace

Rank correlation (Spearman) of views per day with each measure. Right of the line: more of it goes with a higher pace. Zero: no relationship.

Title words

Words in at least 4 titles, by the median pace of those episodes against their neighbors in time. Mostly guest first names, so read it as who, not wording.

Comment mood

VADER sentiment (−1 to +1) averaged over each episode's top comments, mostly jokes and praise, so everything skews positive. Sarcasm and edgy humor read as negative, so a low score does not mean a disliked episode.

Most frequent guests

Guests with more than one interview. Names come from the video descriptions; joint interviews count for each guest. Click a column to sort.

Cumulative views

Running total of views by publish date, in millions.

All episodes

Sort by any column. "Vs neighbors" is views per day divided by the median of the 30 episodes around it.

Guest interviews only: titles with "wings" or "Holiday Extravaganza", 10+ minutes, not host-led or compilations. Data from the YouTube Data API. Code and method on GitHub.
